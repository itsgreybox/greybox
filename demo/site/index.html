"Legacy" doesn't mean abandoned

The most common misconception about this tool: that it's only for dead, untouched systems. Most real legacy pain isn't that — it's code your team ships to every week, where nobody left fully understands why it works the way it does.

🔧Before a risky change

You're about to touch a module you didn't write, that's been "working fine" for years. Scan first — know what's fragile before you edit it, not after it breaks in production.

🧭Onboarding a new engineer

Instead of "ask Dave, he's been here longest," give them a real starting map of what's risky, what's clean, and what to be careful with.

🚨After an incident

"Why did changing the notification service break the payment flow" is a legacy-understanding problem, not a bug — even on a system your team ships to weekly.

📅Quarterly health check

Run it periodically on core modules, not just once before a migration decision — a confidence score that drops over time is an early warning.
